The $300 figure is not a marketing number. It's based on what you actually spend if you eat at
warungs, sleep in budget accommodation, and do the region's best activities without splurging on
anything. I've verified every price in this guide. Nothing here is a rough estimate from a blog
post written in 2019.

Prices verified May 2026.

## What $300 covers in 7 days

- 7 nights accommodation (budget homestay with breakfast)
- 21 meals (mostly warung, a couple of treat meals)
- All transport including getting here from Jakarta
- Green Canyon body rafting
- 3 surf sessions with board rental
- Citumang river tubing
- Scooter rental for 5 days
- Incidentals (water, snacks, SIM card, entry fees)

Not included: the flight or overnight bus cost from your departure point before Jakarta (if you're
coming internationally), alcohol, and souvenirs.

---

## Getting here: Jakarta → Batukaras

**Budget route (Rp 100,000–150,000 one way):**

1. Night bus or train from Jakarta to Banjar (Rp 80,000–130,000 for bus; Rp 90,000–200,000 for
   economy/second-class train).
2. Minivan from Banjar bus terminal to Pangandaran town (Rp 30,000–40,000, shared — runs regularly).
3. Ojek from Pangandaran town to Batukaras (Rp 60,000–80,000 for a private ojek, ~45 minutes) or
   local angkot + ojek combo for Rp 30,000–40,000 if you're patient.

**Budget total for getting here from Jakarta: ~Rp 150,000–200,000 ($9–12 USD).** The overnight bus
handles it in one go if you don't care about comfort.

See [the full transport guide](/guides/how-to-get-from-jakarta-to-batukaras) for detailed booking
instructions.

## Day 1 — Arrive, explore Batukaras, buy your SIM

You've been traveling. Don't try to do anything ambitious.

**Accommodation:** Check into a budget homestay in Batukaras. Budget range is Rp 100,000–150,000/
night with breakfast (toast, eggs, tea — simple but included). Ask for a fan room; AC adds Rp
50,000–80,000/night and isn't necessary if you're used to tropical heat.

**Evening:** Eat at the warung near the main surf break. Nasi goreng with a fried egg, Rp 20,000.
Walk the beach. It takes 15 minutes end to end. The bay is calm at sunset.

**Buy a Telkomsel SIM** if you didn't in Jakarta. The small shop near the main road sells them:
Rp 10,000 for the SIM, Rp 50,000–80,000 for a data package. Essential for maps and navigation.

**Day 1 spend:**

- Accommodation: Rp 130,000
- Food: Rp 45,000 (lunch on the road + dinner)
- SIM + data: Rp 60,000
- **Day total: ~Rp 235,000 (~$15)**

## Day 2 — First surf session

- **Morning:** Rent a board (Rp 75,000 half-day or Rp 100,000 full day). The bay is beginner-
  friendly: sand bottom, slow lefts, easy paddling. If you've never surfed, a 2-hour lesson is
  Rp 200,000–250,000 and genuinely worth it for the first session.
- **Afternoon:** Explore on foot. The rice paddies behind the village are a 10-minute walk.
- **Evening:** The small night market in Batukaras sometimes has grilled corn and pisang goreng —
  Rp 5,000–10,000 per piece.

**Day 2 spend:**

- Accommodation: Rp 130,000
- Board rental: Rp 100,000
- Food: Rp 60,000
- **Day total: ~Rp 290,000 (~$18)**

## Day 3 — Green Canyon

This is the highlight of any Pangandaran trip. Don't skip it to save money — it's the one activity
you'll regret missing.

**Cost:** Rp 250,000 per person. Includes guide, life jacket, helmet, and the experience of
swimming through a limestone gorge with turquoise water. The guide goes first; you follow through
the rock channel. It's safe, beautiful, and unlike anything most people have done.

**Transport:** Either book through your accommodation (they'll arrange a car, roughly Rp 50,000 per
person shared), or rent a scooter and go yourself (30km from Batukaras, 30–40 minutes). Renting
a scooter from today saves money over the next few days.

**Scooter rental:** Rp 75,000–90,000/day. Rent for 5 days (today through Day 7) and you'll
probably get Rp 70,000/day if you negotiate slightly.

**Day 3 spend:**

- Accommodation: Rp 130,000
- Green Canyon: Rp 250,000
- Scooter rental (5 days): Rp 350,000 (paid now, covers through Day 7)
- Fuel: Rp 25,000
- Food: Rp 55,000
- **Day total (including scooter deposit): ~Rp 810,000 (~$51)** — heavy day, but the scooter
  pays off over the next 4 days.

## Day 4 — Pangandaran town + West Beach

Ride your scooter east to Pangandaran town (~45 minutes). This is a different vibe from Batukaras —
bigger, busier, more tourist infrastructure, still cheap.

- Walk **West Beach** (the better of the two main beaches — cleaner, longer). Entry: Rp 10,000.
- Walk the edge of **Cagar Alam Pananjung** nature reserve if time allows — no full hike needed,
  just the entrance area has deer and hornbills.
- **Cheap lunch option in Pangandaran town:** The market area has warung serving full rice meals
  for Rp 15,000–25,000. Better variety than Batukaras for lunch.
- Stock up on snacks, water, and anything you need from Indomaret.

**Day 4 spend:**

- Accommodation: Rp 130,000
- Beach entry + reserve entry: Rp 20,000
- Food: Rp 65,000
- Fuel: Rp 20,000
- Snacks/supplies: Rp 40,000
- **Day total: ~Rp 275,000 (~$17)**

## Day 5 — Citumang river + second surf

- **Morning:** Surf session, board rental Rp 75,000 (half day).
- **Afternoon:** Citumang river tubing (~30 minutes from Batukaras by scooter). Rp 150,000/person
  — you float downstream through jungle, swim in clear pools, see a waterfall section. 2–3 hours
  total. Different from Green Canyon: shallower, more relaxed, good for a hot afternoon.

**Day 5 spend:**

- Accommodation: Rp 130,000
- Board rental: Rp 75,000
- Citumang: Rp 150,000
- Fuel: Rp 15,000
- Food: Rp 65,000
- **Day total: ~Rp 435,000 (~$27)**

## Day 6 — Karapyak Beach + slow day

Karapyak is 30 minutes west of Batukaras, past Batukaras village proper. It's a quiet bay —
no tourist infrastructure, mostly local fishermen, good for swimming and not much else. Free entry.

This is the day for Batukaras's quieter pleasures: take a long lunch at your warung, nap, then
an evening beach walk. You've done the major activities; let the village work on you.

**Treat dinner option:** One of the slightly nicer restaurants on the beach strip does grilled
fish with sambal, rice, and a coconut shake for Rp 90,000–120,000. Worth it once.

**Day 6 spend:**

- Accommodation: Rp 130,000
- Fuel: Rp 20,000
- Food: Rp 120,000 (including treat dinner)
- **Day total: ~Rp 270,000 (~$17)**

## Day 7 — Third surf, then out

- **Morning:** One last surf session. Return the scooter before noon if your checkout is then.
- **Pack and head out.** Arrange your ride to Pangandaran town from your accommodation (Rp
  60,000–80,000 by ojek), then Pangandaran → Banjar → Jakarta in reverse.

**Day 7 spend:**

- Accommodation checkout (already paid)
- Board rental: Rp 75,000
- Transport out to Pangandaran town: Rp 70,000
- Food: Rp 45,000
- **Day total: ~Rp 190,000 (~$12)**

accommodation night or two — or just the peace of mind of not counting every warung meal.

## The rules for staying under budget

1. **Eat at warungs.** This is not a sacrifice — the food is genuinely better. Nasi campur for
   Rp 20,000 beats a tourist restaurant pasta for Rp 100,000 on every metric.
2. **Negotiate accommodation monthly rates** if staying longer than 7 days. The daily rate drops
   significantly.
3. **Book the scooter for multiple days.** Day-rate scooters cost more than weekly negotiated rates.
4. **Don't order at the "tourist beach" restaurants daily.** Once or twice is fine; daily adds up.
5. **Carry cash.** ATMs are in Pangandaran town only; card fees eat into your budget.
