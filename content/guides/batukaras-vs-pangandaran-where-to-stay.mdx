Most first-time visitors assume "Pangandaran" means one place. It doesn&rsquo;t. The town of
Pangandaran is the big beach with the night market and the karaoke bars. Batukaras is roughly
[VERIFY: 45 minutes] west — quiet, surfy, slow.

Picking between them is the single decision that shapes your trip. Here is the honest comparison.

## TL;DR

- **Stay in Batukaras** if you want to surf, you&rsquo;re here for Green Canyon, or "vacation"
  means waking up early and going to bed by ten.
- **Stay in Pangandaran town** if you want food choice, nightlife, easier transport in/out, or
  you&rsquo;re traveling with people who don&rsquo;t surf.
- **Split your stay** if you have four nights or more — most people end up wishing they had.

## Splitting your stay

If you have four nights or more, the math usually favors splitting:

- **Nights 1–2:** Pangandaran town. Eat. Walk. Sleep off the bus.
- **Nights 3+:** Batukaras. Surf. Green Canyon. Reset.
