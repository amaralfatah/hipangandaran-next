## What to bring

- Swimwear under quick-dry clothes
- Reef-safe sunscreen (yes, even in the canyon — sun bounces off the limestone)
- A waterproof phone pouch — the operator photos are usually [VERIFY: extra Rp price]
- Cash for tipping the guide
