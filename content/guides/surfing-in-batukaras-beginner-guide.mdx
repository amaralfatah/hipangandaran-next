## Etiquette (read this)

- The lineup is forgiving but not infinite. Don&rsquo;t paddle for every wave.
- Locals get priority. Watch for ten minutes before joining.
- Don&rsquo;t ditch your board on the way out — there are people behind you.

## What to bring

- Reef-safe sunscreen, lots of it
- Rashie — long-sleeve if your back burns
- Cash — most rentals don&rsquo;t take cards
