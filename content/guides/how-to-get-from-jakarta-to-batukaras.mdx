Most travel blogs hand-wave the Jakarta → Batukaras journey with "take a bus, it's easy." It is
not easy on a first try. There are three reasonable routes, and which one you pick depends on
whether you care more about your back, your wallet, or your time.

This guide walks through all three with the actual numbers — including the Cijulang back road that
saves roughly an hour if your driver knows it.

## What to bring

- Cash in small denominations — most rural minivans don&rsquo;t take cards or QRIS.
- Offline Google Maps for the Pangandaran → Batukaras leg.
- Motion-sickness pills if you&rsquo;re sensitive — the last 20 km is winding.

## What this guide doesn&rsquo;t cover

- Flights to Bandung + onward transport (rarely faster door-to-door).
- Renting a car from Jakarta (only worth it for groups of 4+).
- Routes from Bali / Yogyakarta — separate guide.
