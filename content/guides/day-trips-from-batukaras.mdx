Batukaras is small. You can walk the entire beach strip in 15 minutes. That's part of what makes
it appealing — but if you're staying more than 3–4 days, you'll want to get out.

The good news: most of the region's best experiences are within a 30–60 minute scooter ride.
Here are the five that are worth the effort, ordered by how strongly I'd recommend them.

---

## 1. Green Canyon (Cukang Taneuh) — 30 minutes west

**What it is:** Body rafting through a limestone gorge with turquoise-to-emerald water. You swim
through narrow channels between walls of rock, guided by a local who's done it hundreds of times.
The gorge is spectacular — water-carved stone, overhanging jungle, shafts of light hitting the
surface. It's the highlight of the Pangandaran region.

**Logistics:**

- Distance: ~30km from Batukaras, fully paved road
- Get there: Scooter (easiest), or book a car through your accommodation (shared, ~Rp 50,000)
- Cost: Rp 250,000/person, includes guide, life jacket, and helmet
- Duration: 3–4 hours including transport
- Best time: Dry season (April–October) for clearest water

**Don't skip:** Ask your guide to point out where the water changes colour as you go deeper. The
transition from murky river to clear canyon water is abrupt and dramatic.

See [the full Green Canyon guide](/guides/green-canyon-body-rafting-complete-guide) for complete
details on what to bring and what to expect.

## 2. Citumang River — 25 minutes north

**What it is:** A calm, clear river swimming and tubing experience in jungle surroundings. Less
dramatic than Green Canyon, more relaxed. You float downstream through rock formations, swim in
natural pools, pass a small waterfall. Good for an afternoon rather than a full day.

**Logistics:**

- Distance: ~20km from Batukaras, road is mostly good with one rough section
- Get there: Scooter or ojek (Rp 30,000–50,000 each way)
- Cost: Rp 150,000/person, includes life jacket and brief guide
- Duration: 2–3 hours
- Best time: Dry season, but it operates year-round

**Good combination:** Do Green Canyon on Day 1, Citumang on Day 3. They're complementary, not
redundant — different enough to be worth both if you have the time.

## 3. Pangandaran town + West Beach — 45 minutes east

**What it is:** The main tourist hub of Kabupaten Pangandaran. More developed, louder, and
significantly less pretty than Batukaras — but useful for supplies, a change of pace, and the
beach itself which is longer and sandier than Batukaras.

West Beach is the better of the two main beaches. The water isn't great for swimming (strong
undertow in places), but the sand walk is good. The market area behind the beach has the best
warung variety in the region.

**Also worth seeing:**

- The edge of **Cagar Alam Pananjung** (the nature reserve, half-day hike — covered separately
  in the [full hiking guide](/guides/cagar-alam-pananjung-hiking-guide))
- The fishing village at the south tip of the peninsula — active, photogenic, and very local

**Logistics:**

- Distance: ~45km from Batukaras
- Get there: Your own scooter (best) or shared angkot from Cijulang junction (~Rp 20,000 + waiting)
- Cost: Rp 15,000–25,000 entry to the beach/reserve area
- Duration: Half day minimum to do it properly
- Bring: Cash (ATM here is the only one near Batukaras), any supplies you need

## 4. Karapyak Beach — 20 minutes west of Batukaras

**What it is:** A quiet bay west of Batukaras, past the main village, with almost no tourist
infrastructure. Local fishing boats pulled up on the sand, a handful of locals swimming on
weekends, no hawkers, no warungs to speak of. Go for the swimming and the silence.

The water is calmer than the open coast here — the bay provides some protection. Good for swimming
even when the surf at Batukaras is choppy.

**Logistics:**

- Distance: ~15km from Batukaras
- Get there: Scooter on a quiet coastal road through coconut groves
- Cost: Free
- Duration: 1–3 hours — it's a place to linger, not tick off

**Honest note:** There's not much here beyond the beach itself. That's the point. If you need
something to do, this isn't it. If you want to sit on a beach with no one trying to sell you
anything, this is exactly it.

**Combine with:** [The coastal road to Karapyak](/guides/batukaras-digital-nomad-week-itinerary)
is genuinely scenic — budget extra time for the ride itself.

## 5. Batu Hiu — 30 minutes southwest

**What it is:** A cliffside headland jutting into the Indian Ocean south of Pangandaran, named
for its shark-fin shape. The views are dramatic — open ocean on three sides, crashing surf far
below, and a small prayer shrine at the point. No beach, no swimming (the cliffs drop straight
into rough water), but the scenery is genuinely striking.

**Logistics:**

- Distance: ~20km from Batukaras via a winding coastal road
- Get there: Scooter — the road is scenic and worth taking slowly
- Cost: Rp 10,000–15,000 entry fee
- Duration: 1–2 hours

**Good pairing:** Batu Hiu + Karapyak in one afternoon scooter loop. Batu Hiu for the scenery,
Karapyak for the swim. Total loop is about 50km from Batukaras.

## What's not on this list

**Madasari Beach** (farther west, ~1 hour) is sometimes recommended. It's beautiful but the access
road is rough and the experience is similar to Karapyak — a quieter, emptier beach. Worth it if
you have an extra day or a motorbike that handles dirt roads.

**Santirah and Jojogan** are accessible but genuinely remote. Don't go without asking locally
about road conditions and whether anything is actually open.
