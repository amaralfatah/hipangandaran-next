on a bus. More and you&rsquo;ll start hitting diminishing returns unless you surf.

## What this itinerary skips

- **Citumang.** Beautiful but redundant if you&rsquo;re doing Green Canyon. Save it for a return
  trip.
- **Karapyak.** Tier 2 destination — best added if you have 5+ days.
- **Cagar Alam Pananjung nature reserve.** Skip on a 3-day trip; it&rsquo;s a half-day in itself
  and competes with Green Canyon for energy.

## Adapt this for your group

- **With non-surfers:** swap Day 2 morning for a half-day at Cagar Alam Pananjung instead.
- **With kids:** stay 2 nights in Pangandaran town (less transit), do Green Canyon as a day trip,
  skip Batukaras unless someone surfs.
- **Solo digital nomad:** flip it — Batukaras for nights 1–3 (stable WiFi at [VERIFY: cafe name]),
  Pangandaran town only on the way out.
